# CHRONOS demo: voiceover script

Timed to `chronos-demo-silent.mp4` (65 s). Read it calmly and precisely, like a product film rather than an advert. Use one voice, leave short pauses, and don't hype anything.

**Voice brief:** a calm, warm, mid-low voice with a neutral or light British accent. It should sound unhurried and exact, matching the Swiss-editorial look: off-white, black type, one orange accent.
